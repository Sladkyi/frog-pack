# Epic attacks

Five compact 12-frame attacks generated with built-in ImageGen. Style reference: beam-frames-v2.png. Sources and optimized WebP copies are saved in assets and assets/optimized. Not yet assigned to gameplay weapons.

Layout: 4 columns, 3 rows; fixed scale and registration. Preview: epic-attacks-preview.html.

## Prompts
