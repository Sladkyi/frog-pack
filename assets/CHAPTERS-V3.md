# Three chapters and seed bow

21 encounters, seven per chapter; bosses at 7, 14 and 21. Existing forest guardian, new mushroom king and ancient tree. Six new regular enemy types: armored moss beetle, regenerating mushroom, charging boar, fast hovering moth, heavy-hitting stump, slow armored crystal snail. Each new type has two drawn poses. Chapter palettes and enemy pools change with progression.
